# Risk register

Score = Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5). Levels: **High ≥ 15 · Medium 8–14 · Low ≤ 7.**
The level decides the techniques and the depth of testing (right-hand columns). Scores were set in the planning step (26 Nov, before test execution) and are re-assessed as *residual risk* in the completion report.
